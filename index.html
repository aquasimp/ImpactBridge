About the Platform

We blend AI, Maps & Cloud to solve the volunteer coordination crisis.

India has 3.4 million NGOs, but 89% of their field data is collected on paper and never digitized. Volunteers are abundant but poorly matched to actual needs. ImpactBridge closes this gap with Gemini AI-powered data extraction, intelligent skill matching, and real-time geospatial coordination — turning scattered efforts into organized impact.
